About

DJ Larmzy is a UK-based Afrobeats DJ with a growing reputation for high-energy sets that keep dancefloors moving from the first track to the last. Rooted in Afrobeats, Amapiano and Afro-house, Larmzy blends the sounds of West and Southern Africa with Hip-Hop, R&B and Dancehall, reading every room and tailoring each set to the crowd in front of him. Born Joseph Alamu, Larmzy hails from Nigeria, where he was first drawn to music at a very young age. What began as playing music for friends and family at church camp dinners soon became a serious passion. Largely self-taught, he sharpened his craft through hours of practice, studying mixes from DJs he admired, and learning the art of transitions, set planning and song selection. While studying Data Science and Business Analysis at the University of Plymouth, Larmzy took his sound to university events, student nights, house parties, clubs and and bar building a loyal following among audiences hungry for authentic African sounds in the South West. Since then he has played clubs, private events, weddings and birthday parties, and is now expanding into open-format sets that move effortlessly between Afrobeats, Hip-Hop, R&B, Dancehall, Soca and Pop. Behind the decks, Larmzy brings the same discipline and attention to detail that runs through everything he does, combined with an infectious energy that turns any crowd into a party. With new mixes on the way and his brand growing on Instagram (@djlarmzy), DJ Larmzy is a name to watch on the UK Afro scene.
